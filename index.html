Garlic butter pasta

Standard kit20 min

Stockpot + frying pan · Serves 2

Ingredients and steps

You need

  • 8 oz spaghetti
  • 3 tbsp butter or olive oil
  • 4 cloves garlic, sliced (or 1 tsp garlic powder)
  • Italian herb blend, salt, black pepper
  • Parmesan if you have it

Steps

  1. Boil a big pot of salted water and cook the pasta 1 minute less than the box says. Save a mug of pasta water.
  2. Warm the butter or oil in the frying pan on medium-low. Add garlic and cook until just golden, about 2 minutes.
  3. Add the drained pasta, a splash of pasta water and a big pinch of herbs. Toss until glossy. Salt and pepper to taste.

Weeknight fried rice

Full kit15 min

Deep sauté pan · Serves 2

Ingredients and steps

You need

  • 2 cups cooked rice (day-old is best)
  • 1 chicken breast, diced, or 2 eggs
  • 2 cups frozen mixed vegetables
  • 1 tbsp neutral oil + 1 tsp toasted sesame oil
  • Soy sauce, garlic powder

Steps

  1. Heat the neutral oil in the sauté pan on medium-high. Cook the chicken until no longer pink, about 5 minutes.
  2. Add the vegetables and cook 3 minutes. Push everything aside and scramble the eggs if using.
  3. Add the rice, soy sauce and a pinch of garlic powder. Stir-fry until the rice is hot and a little crispy. Finish with sesame oil.

Sheet-pan chicken and vegetables

Full kit35 min

Baking sheet · Serves 2 to 3

Ingredients and steps

You need

  • 4 bone-in chicken thighs
  • 1 head broccoli, 2 carrots, 2 potatoes, chopped
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • Paprika, garlic powder, salt, black pepper

Steps

  1. Heat the oven to 425°F. Toss the vegetables with half the oil and seasonings on the baking sheet.
  2. Rub the chicken with the rest of the oil and seasonings and nestle it on top, skin side up.
  3. Roast 30 to 35 minutes until the chicken reaches 165°F inside and the vegetables are browned.

Perfect fried eggs

Mini kit5 min

Frying pan · Serves 1

Ingredients and steps

You need

  • 2 eggs
  • 1 tsp oil or butter
  • Salt and black pepper

Steps

  1. Warm the frying pan on medium-low with the oil for a minute.
  2. Crack in the eggs. Cook without moving them until the whites are set, 3 to 4 minutes.
  3. Season and slide onto a plate. Want them over-easy? Flip with the spatula for 20 seconds.

Fluffy stovetop rice

Mini kit25 min

Saucepan · Serves 2

Ingredients and steps

You need

  • 1 cup long-grain white rice
  • 1½ cups water
  • Pinch of salt

Steps

  1. Rinse the rice until the water runs mostly clear.
  2. Add rice, water and salt to the saucepan. Bring to a boil, then lid on and heat to low.
  3. Cook 15 minutes, turn off the heat and let it sit 5 more minutes with the lid on. Fluff with a fork.

Big-batch pasta night

Standard kit25 min

Stockpot · Serves 4 to 6

Ingredients and steps

You need

  • 1 lb pasta
  • 1 jar marinara
  • 1 lb ground beef or a can of chickpeas
  • Italian herb blend, chili flakes

Steps

  1. Boil the pasta in a well-salted stockpot.
  2. While it cooks, brown the beef (or warm the chickpeas) in the frying pan, then add marinara and herbs.
  3. Drain the pasta, toss it with the sauce in the stockpot, and serve straight from the pot.
