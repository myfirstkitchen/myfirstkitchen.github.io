Questions

Can I pick a different schedule for each item?

Yes. Each oil or seasoning has its own frequency, from every 2 weeks up to every 6 months. Items that ship on the same schedule are grouped into one box.

Can I skip or cancel?

You can skip a delivery, change the schedule or cancel an item at any time before it ships.

How much do I save?

Subscribed items are 10% off the one-time price.

Do I need to buy a kit first?

No. Subscriptions work on their own, or alongside a kit from the builder.
