Questions about refills

Can I pick a different schedule for each item?

Yes. Every oil, salt or spice has its own frequency, from every 2 weeks to every 6 months. Items on the same schedule ship together in one box.

Can I skip or cancel?

You can skip a shipment, change an item’s schedule or cancel it at any time before it ships.

How much do I save?

Subscribed items are 10% off the one-time price.

Do I need to buy a kit first?

No. Refills work on their own, or alongside a kit from the kit builder.
